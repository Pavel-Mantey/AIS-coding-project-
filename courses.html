COURSES OFFERED IN AIS

In AIS, students can choose to offer the following courses:

  • General Science
  • General Arts
  • Business
  • Home Economics
  • Visual Arts

HOW THE COURSES ARE DISTRIBUTED AMONG THE CLASSES

The classes(SHS) are labelled from A to F. The science students are distributed in the A, B and C classes. The D and E classes house the General Arts students. The F class has the Business, Home Economics and Visual Arts students
